# Coldharbour — game design document
Target: morrowind. Status: preproduction/design, no playable release verified.

## Vision
A rupture draws the player into an incomplete and hostile reflection of familiar Vvardenfell places; its inhabitants are trapped by contracts and imposed identities rather than simply identical copies of Tamriel. The player investigates why fragments repeat, makes alliances with escapees and rival wardens, and chooses whether to sever the mechanism, redirect it or use its bargains at a cost. Avoid rewriting the main game's history or presenting invented cosmology as established lore. Player loop: explore corrupted landmarks, recover testimonies, negotiate obligations, unlock passages, free or abandon captives. Three proposed hub types: refuge, ruined market and fortress. Vertical slice: distorted settlement, refuge, ruined shrine, chained passage; dialogue/journal quest with a noncombat resolution. Native TES3 identity, LAND/CELL, journal/dialogue, MWScript, pathgrids and legacy NIF are gates.


## Player experience
Contracts, memory, exploration, dialogue, escape. Opening: A Place Remembered. Provide combat and noncombat solutions where appropriate; optional content must reward exploration rather than pad playtime.

## Scope and continuity
Text-first dialogue; do not present invented realm details as established lore.
Minimum targets: 12 main quests, 40 side quests, 50 destinations, 35 enterable interiors, 15 substantial dungeons, 75 interior cells, 25 principal characters, 60 supporting NPCs, 130 original asset designs; 12–18 main hours and 35–50 total hours, measured only after playable tests. These are floors; the Flagstaff earlier larger scope is retained.
