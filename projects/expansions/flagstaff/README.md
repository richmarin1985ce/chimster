# Fallout: Flagstaff — game design document
Target: newvegas. Status: preproduction/design, no playable release verified.

## Vision
Preserve the existing seven-quest scaffold and original characters in examples/flagstaff/cast-and-quest-outline.md. The Courier is abducted into Legion territory; wartime requisition turns Flagstaff's civilian supply economy into a struggle over ownership of roads, labor, food and trade. The hidden reconnaissance subplot remains optional; no unilateral Chicago Enclave canon. Player loop: captivity, escape or negotiated release, caravan jobs, investigations, political bargaining, regional consequences. Major outcomes vary by captive freedom, community survival and road access. Vertical slice: pit entrance, labor yard, guarded supply store, escape corridor, roadside repair shop; confiscation ledger, Ash, two alternative escape routes, gear recovery and exit. Geography: retain provisional existing 8 km source and add TIGER roads later; original bounds not recovered. Native FNV world/interior, QUST/DIAL/INFO, script compilation and NAVM/NAVI release gate.


## Player experience
Civilian commerce, captivity, political negotiation, exploration. Opening: The Roman Holiday. Provide combat and noncombat solutions where appropriate; optional content must reward exploration rather than pad playtime.

## Scope and continuity
No existing New Vegas character appearances; 2281; independent merchant slavers, not Legion soldiers; mandatory opening CR_MQ01 The Roman Holiday; Chicago reconnaissance optional. Preserve original 80–120 location / 150–250 named NPC ambition as a larger working target, not a cap.
Minimum targets: 12 main quests, 40 side quests, 50 destinations, 35 enterable interiors, 15 substantial dungeons, 75 interior cells, 25 principal characters, 60 supporting NPCs, 130 original asset designs; 12–18 main hours and 35–50 total hours, measured only after playable tests. These are floors; the Flagstaff earlier larger scope is retained.
