# The Hunting Grounds — game design document
Target: oblivion. Status: preproduction/design, no playable release verified.

## Vision
A disappearance in the Great Forest exposes competing hunters and werewolves; an invitation or forced crossing leads to an island-like hunting realm. The hunt is a contested ritual: who defines prey, who can refuse and what transformation costs. Rival packs, mortal hunters and a neutral refuge complicate the choices. Player loop: tracking, investigation, stalking, survival, transformation and escape; allow a non-werewolf route and multiple hunt outcomes. Vertical slice: Great Forest camp, trail, crossing, island lodge and hunt arena; one transformation trial and an alternative tracking solution. Classic TES4 first; Remastered is deferred. Native world, pathgrid, creature/animation, scripts and LAND/LOD gates.


## Player experience
Tracking, ritual trials, consensual transformation, exploration. Opening: The Empty Camp. Provide combat and noncombat solutions where appropriate; optional content must reward exploration rather than pad playtime.

## Scope and continuity
Classic Oblivion first; Remastered separate/deferred; non-werewolf route.
Minimum targets: 12 main quests, 40 side quests, 50 destinations, 35 enterable interiors, 15 substantial dungeons, 75 interior cells, 25 principal characters, 60 supporting NPCs, 130 original asset designs; 12–18 main hours and 35–50 total hours, measured only after playable tests. These are floors; the Flagstaff earlier larger scope is retained.
