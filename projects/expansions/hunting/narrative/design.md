# The Hunting Grounds — narrative design
Review snapshot, 2026-11-28. All additions remain proposals unless creator-locked. Existing canonical quest IDs retained.

## Factions
- **Ashen Track Pack** (pack): Protect kin and refuse coerced hunts
- **Antler Company** (hunters): Treat the hunt as status and obligation
- **Hearthkeepers** (refuge): Shelter mortals and transformed people
- **Keepers of the Chase** (ritual): Preserve ritual rules; divided on voluntary participation

## Principal cast
- **Maelin Varo** (maelin): Missing-person investigator and guide. Faction: refuge; home: camp.
- **Corin Hale** (corin): Hunter who treats quarry as trophies. Faction: hunters; home: lodge.
- **Siv Redtrail** (siv): Pack leader seeking a ceasefire. Faction: pack; home: packden.
- **Lenna Thorne** (len): Healer testing ways to manage transformation. Faction: refuge; home: hearth.
- **Orren Reed** (orr): Ritual interpreter concerned with fair terms. Faction: ritual; home: arena.
- **Bren Ashfoot** (bren): Scout skeptical of outsiders. Faction: pack; home: trail.
- **Fara Glen** (fara): Hunter who wants to leave the company. Faction: hunters; home: watch.
- **Tessa Venn** (tess): Refuge keeper collecting food and supplies. Faction: refuge; home: mill.
