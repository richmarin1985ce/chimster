# The Pits — game design document
Target: skyrim. Status: preproduction/design, no playable release verified.

## Vision
Peryite's realm is a mineral-scarred basin with a central impact-like abyss. A spreading affliction on the mortal side leads to a realm where competing custodians insist that different forms of order are necessary for survival. Six proposed regional districts surround the abyss (not a settled lore claim): salt terraces, ash channels, fungal galleries, drowned mineral works, wind-cut ledges and an inhabited quarantine refuge. Player loop: explore, diagnose environmental threats, obtain protective equipment, negotiate with custodians and choose how to contain or redirect the affliction. Vertical slice: entry shrine, quarantine refuge, toxic terrace, descent gate and one hazard dungeon. Native Skyrim quest aliases, Papyrus/VMAD, hazard records, world and navigation gates. Keep disease effects fictional and mechanically legible.


## Player experience
Hazard navigation, exploration, fictional disease mechanics, diplomacy. Opening: A Measure of Disorder. Provide combat and noncombat solutions where appropriate; optional content must reward exploration rather than pad playtime.

## Scope and continuity
Six districts proposed; do not claim canon; keep fictional mechanics legible.
Minimum targets: 12 main quests, 40 side quests, 50 destinations, 35 enterable interiors, 15 substantial dungeons, 75 interior cells, 25 principal characters, 60 supporting NPCs, 130 original asset designs; 12–18 main hours and 35–50 total hours, measured only after playable tests. These are floors.
