# Fallout 66 — period-reference ledger
Creator directive: Fallout: Flagstaff uses period-appropriate references to music, films and other culture, but should not exhaust the pool for future Fallout 66 installments. Fallout 66 is the series identity, not Flagstaff's standalone title.

## Editorial rules
Use primarily mid-century American cultural references consistent with Fallout's retrofuturist setting. Use a proposed 1930s–early 1960s research window as a starting point, not a hard creator-imposed cutoff or an assertion that all such references are canon. Verify date and thematic relevance before accepting any new real reference. No modern jokes disguised as historical references; no post-2281 story knowledge in Flagstaff. Prefer original diegetic advertisements, fictional songs, businesses, films and radio scripts; no copied lyrics, dialogue, recordings, album art, actor likeness or unlicensed audio. Track actual asset rights separately from references and titles. A period title allusion does not authorize use of a work.
