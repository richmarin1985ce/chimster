# Panda world-space volumetric lighting
Native Panda and Panda Filters automatically choose the GLSL volume path when height-dependent fog, local fog volumes or scattering with fog is requested (water remains separate). It reuses ModernGL's bounded Beer-Lambert integration, local point/spot radiance and Henyey-Greenstein phase approximation. Native fog is disabled for the duration of the volume pass to prevent double extinction. Sky pixels are integrated out to the configured distance. A single auxiliary orthographic depth map, capped at 512 pixels, supplies sun visibility; local-light shadows are not sampled. The original native/PBR water paths and SimplePBR post graph remain separate. No paid dependencies.
