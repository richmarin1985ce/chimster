# Panda world-space volumetric lighting
Native Panda and Panda Filters automatically choose the GLSL volume path when height-dependent fog, local fog volumes or scattering with fog is requested (water remains separate). It reuses ModernGL's bounded Beer-Lambert integration, local point/spot radiance and Henyey-Greenstein phase approximation. Native fog is disabled for the duration of the volume pass to prevent double extinction. Sky pixels are integrated out to the configured distance. A single auxiliary orthographic depth map, capped at 512 pixels, supplies sun visibility; local-light shadows are not sampled. SimplePBR inserts the same volume/AO shader into its existing HDR filter graph ahead of tone mapping. Water paths remain separate pending combined testing. Local fog intersections are clipped analytically against each ray segment so small regions are not skipped by coarse samples. No paid dependencies.

Above-water planar reflection/refraction now runs ahead of the PBR scene and volume buffers, with water excluded from auxiliary cameras and depth writes enabled when refraction is active. Underwater transitions retain native fog. Transparent water without refraction remains approximate because it does not write depth.
