# Expansion production briefs — 2026-11-28

Creator approved the six-project production direction and geographic sourcing work. All newly introduced story beats and names below are proposals, not final canon. Existing Flagstaff cast/quest outline remains authoritative. All six are creator-directed full DLC-scale projects, comparable in ambition to Dragonborn or Shivering Isles. Planning targets per expansion: 12–18 hours main story, 35–50 total authored gameplay, 12 main quests, at least 40 side quests, at least 50 authored locations, 25 principal characters and 60 supporting NPCs. These are proposed production budgets, not measurements. No proprietary assets are bundled. Each project first delivers a 30–60 minute playable vertical slice.

## Fallout 66: Courier's Road (New Vegas)
Preserve the existing seven-quest scaffold and original characters in examples/flagstaff/cast-and-quest-outline.md. The Courier is abducted into Legion territory; wartime requisition turns Flagstaff's civilian supply economy into a struggle over ownership of roads, labor, food and trade. The hidden reconnaissance subplot remains optional; no unilateral Chicago Enclave canon. Player loop: captivity, escape or negotiated release, caravan jobs, investigations, political bargaining, regional consequences. Major outcomes vary by captive freedom, community survival and road access. Vertical slice: pit entrance, labor yard, guarded supply store, escape corridor, roadside repair shop; confiscation ledger, Ash, two alternative escape routes, gear recovery and exit. Geography: retain provisional existing 8 km source and add TIGER roads later; original bounds not recovered. Native FNV world/interior, QUST/DIAL/INFO, script compilation and NAVM/NAVI release gate.

## Coldharbour (Morrowind)
A rupture draws the player into an incomplete and hostile reflection of familiar Vvardenfell places; its inhabitants are trapped by contracts and imposed identities rather than simply identical copies of Tamriel. The player investigates why fragments repeat, makes alliances with escapees and rival wardens, and chooses whether to sever the mechanism, redirect it or use its bargains at a cost. Avoid rewriting the main game's history or presenting invented cosmology as established lore. Player loop: explore corrupted landmarks, recover testimonies, negotiate obligations, unlock passages, free or abandon captives. Three proposed hub types: refuge, ruined market and fortress. Vertical slice: distorted settlement, refuge, ruined shrine, chained passage; dialogue/journal quest with a noncombat resolution. Native TES3 identity, LAND/CELL, journal/dialogue, MWScript, pathgrids and legacy NIF are gates.

## The Hunting Grounds (classic Oblivion)
A disappearance in the Great Forest exposes competing hunters and werewolves; an invitation or forced crossing leads to an island-like hunting realm. The hunt is a contested ritual: who defines prey, who can refuse and what transformation costs. Rival packs, mortal hunters and a neutral refuge complicate the choices. Player loop: tracking, investigation, stalking, survival, transformation and escape; allow a non-werewolf route and multiple hunt outcomes. Vertical slice: Great Forest camp, trail, crossing, island lodge and hunt arena; one transformation trial and an alternative tracking solution. Classic TES4 first; Remastered is deferred. Native world, pathgrid, creature/animation, scripts and LAND/LOD gates.

## The Pits (Skyrim)
Peryite's realm is a mineral-scarred basin with a central impact-like abyss. A spreading affliction on the mortal side leads to a realm where competing custodians insist that different forms of order are necessary for survival. Six proposed regional districts surround the abyss (not a settled lore claim): salt terraces, ash channels, fungal galleries, drowned mineral works, wind-cut ledges and an inhabited quarantine refuge. Player loop: explore, diagnose environmental threats, obtain protective equipment, negotiate with custodians and choose how to contain or redirect the affliction. Vertical slice: entry shrine, quarantine refuge, toxic terrace, descent gate and one hazard dungeon. Native Skyrim quest aliases, Papyrus/VMAD, hazard records, world and navigation gates. Keep disease effects fictional and mechanically legible.

## Fallout: Baltimore (Fallout 3)
The harbor is a contested artery for salvage and provisions. A broken transit route draws the player into a conflict between dock cooperatives, protection crews and inland communities over who controls access to water and transport. Original factions, no required changes to Capital Wasteland endings. Player loop: navigate city blocks, restore or reroute infrastructure, investigate salvage claims, choose alliances, explore subterranean routes. Proposed hubs: harbor market, industrial salvage yard, rowhouse refuge, inland transit junction. Vertical slice: arrival route, one harbor block, warehouse, service tunnel, refuge; a repair-or-negotiation quest and two routes to the exit. Real geographic source: existing terrain-plan bbox; compress regional distances while preserving distinctive street relationships and harbor shape. Native FO3 world, records, scripts, urban occlusion and navigation gates.

## Fallout: Nautica (Fallout 4)
On Nantucket, competing coastal communities depend on a deteriorating maritime supply network. A failed crossing leads the player to investigate missing cargo and decide how to restore access without giving one faction unchecked control. Original proposed factions: harbor cooperative, salvage flotilla, inland refuge. Player loop: coastal exploration, wreck salvage, supply restoration, settlements and branching negotiations. Proposed hubs: town harbor, inland crossroads, eastern coastal station, sheltered fishing community. Vertical slice: arrival dock, two streets, boathouse, marsh path, beacon station; rescue/supply quest and two resolution paths. Island geography preserved more than metropolitan Baltimore; offshore travel and bathymetry are separate designed layers. Native FO4 world, water, Papyrus/VMAD, materials, collision/navmesh and previs gates.

## Common production contracts
- Separate source geography, playable design geometry and native game records. Source data never silently becomes playable content.
- Assign stable project IDs before authoring quests, locations, actors, and assets. Track prerequisite and outcome graphs. Each vertical slice must have entry, resolution, return/exit, save/load and missing-dependency checks.
- AI-generated drafts require schema validation, provenance and human review; transaction previews and rollback before native writes. Keep the native manual workflow available.
- Geographic source recipes and downloaded data are distinct milestones. Record URL, hash, projection, source date/version, masks, scale, and original-vs-authored transformations.
- Do not call an expansion playable until verified in its target game. A preview is not a plugin.

## Content scale and quality gate (2026-11-28)
The structured campaign-board.json now contains 7 main and 20 side quest outlines per project, including linked optional arcs. Preserve existing IDs and canon. Do not count repeated procedural objectives as unique authored quests. Each optional storyline must have a distinct player motivation, discovery or interaction, meaningful choice, resolution, reward and appropriate consequence. Budget per project: 4–7 distinct hubs; at least 4 optional multi-quest chains; at least 12 optional dungeon/landmark experiences; enough exploration, environmental storytelling and encounter variety to justify the intended scale. These overlap quest/location totals, not additive guarantees. Measure active play time in independent runs after implementation, not by multiplying fetch quests or traversal distance. Large worlds should remain dense and fun, with hand-authored landmarks and noncombat solutions. All proposals require lore/continuity review.

## Enterable world and dungeon production standard
All six expansions must include substantial explorable interiors, ordinary buildings, and dungeons. Initial production minimum per expansion: 50 named destinations including at least 35 enterable interiors, at least 15 substantial dungeons, and 75 interior cells (not 75 unique destinations). These are floors, not caps. Existing named interiors and newly listed destinations overlap the 50-location inventory; do not double count connected rooms or levels as distinct dungeons. Urban projects should exceed the minimum where density warrants it. Every planned enterable destination needs an entrance and exit, a meaningful discovery or activity, navigation/collision, lighting, appropriate NPC or encounter placement, loot or narrative purpose, and a verified save/load path. Dungeons should include distinct layouts, optional discoveries, route variation and meaningful rewards; avoid filler. Parent-location links are design associations, not verified native door links. Architectural grayboxes and authored interiors remain separate from native implementation and runtime testing.
