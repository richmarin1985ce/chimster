# Roman Holidays

## Locked creative canon — user directive
Roman Holidays is the mandatory opening quest of the Fallout: New Vegas Route 66 / Flagstaff expansion. Preserve this exact title and opening position in all future creative passes, quest graphs, scripts and release plans. Do not rename or demote it. It serves as the expansion entry hook analogous in function, not copied content, to Old World Blues' Midnight Science Fiction Feature!.

Its promise of hospitality and civilization contrasts with the Legion's capital and the cost of its order.
