# Flagstaff creative pass 01
Provisional except locked titles. 2281; no vanilla character appearances. Independent merchant slavers abduct the Courier; Legion authorities are not the kidnappers.

## CR_MQ01 The Roman Holiday
A pre-War travel recording plays on a restored roadside loudspeaker at a fictional Mojave motor court, the Last Resort. A caravan broker offers a paid eastbound escort job. The travel confirmation is explicit; the abduction occurs after departure, not through an unavoidable ambient trigger. Fade to captivity, no prescribed player response. A confiscation receipt lists recoverable inventory; never silently destroy quest items. The first playable room is a converted motor-court laundry below a fenced freight yard. Ash asks whether the Courier can read the receipt: "They charged me for the rope. Apparently I rented it." End stage when the player learns the exit routes and gains control of their escape plan. CR_MQ02 owns the escape itself.
## CR_MQ02 Don't Fence Me In
Three routes: expose fraudulent freight accounting to a civilian clerk (Barter/Speech), repair a disused laundry lift (Repair), or organize a captive breakout (Sneak/combat). A low-skill fallback is an expensive work-debt route. Each has consequences for Ash, other captives, witnesses and equipment recovery. No mandatory killing. Recover equipment from a tagged secure inventory; test quest-item behavior and companion handling before shipping.
## CR_MQ03 All Roads Lead to Rome
Nell Fallow's repair shop serves as the crossroads. Three contracts reveal the same requisition seal: a missing ranch shipment, a disputed toll and a falsified captive ledger. Any two establish the trail to Vesta Marrow's caravan house. All three unlock a stronger negotiating position. Player learns that civilian prosperity depends on both protected commerce and coerced labor. No single faction speech resolves the conflict.

- The Straight and Narrow: tollhouse on a reclaimed road cut, painted lane arrows, inspection canopy and merchant seals.
- Fallow's Fine Motor Service: pre-War service station with hand-painted replacement signage, repair bench and road memorabilia.
- The Exchange: capital trade hall with original Roman-inspired administrative banners, shaded stalls, weighing station and separate captive labor access.
