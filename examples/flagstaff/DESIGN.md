# Fallout Route 66: Flagstaff — Courier's Road
Status: user-established direction plus provisional creative proposals, 2026-11-26. Standalone fan expansion for Fallout: New Vegas, built with Chimster. No official affiliation. Names may evolve.
## Creative requirements
- 2281, contemporary with the events of New Vegas and before its final battle. No direct appearances by existing New Vegas characters. References to established history are allowed; do not assume any post-2281 events or later franchise outcomes.
- The Courier is kidnapped in the Mojave by independent merchant slavers, not Legion soldiers, and transported to a slave pit in the Flagstaff region. Escape is the opening act. The player subsequently gains access to merchants, stores, ranchers and local society through branching choices.
- Flagstaff is the Legion capital, a prosperous civilian trade hub under coercive military authority. Legal shops cannot sell liquor or prohibited chems. Show trade security, bighorner ranching, affluence and local culture alongside slavery, repression, and unequal legal status. Prosperity is not universal and does not excuse the system.
- Legion politics are the primary conflict; a small original reconnaissance cell claiming Chicago Enclave ties is a secondary thread. Route 66, the Mother Road, is the central geographic, economic and thematic spine, with exits and connections for future installments including Chicago.
- Target New Vegas-scale density, length and exploration, packaged like an optional DLC with an entrance, independent worldspace, return route and conditional effects on the Mojave endgame. No forced selection of a vanilla ending. No modern real-world businesses, people or contemporary political references; adapt historical geographic/road references to Fallout's divergent setting.
- All new characters, dialogue, textures and creative assets are original mod material or authorized user-owned game dependencies. No actor voice cloning.
## Provisional world design
The Mother Road is an arterial caravan route. Six candidate regions: western caravan approach and slave pit; capital/market district; ranching uplands; timber and cinder country; old motor courts and industrial ruins; eastern relay corridor. Preserve actual elevation source before artistic edits. Provisional 8 km public DEM is a test fixture, not the final worldspace boundary. City map and settlement names evolve.
## Provisional quest structure (spoiler-light)
1. Captive: negotiate, organize escape, work off a fraudulent debt, or break out; restore confiscated equipment through a recoverable inventory system. Multiple viable player builds and original NPCs.
2. The Road: earn independent standing through merchants, ranchers and ordinary residents; uncover competing interests behind the abduction.
3. Capital politics: intersecting civilian commercial, military, administrative and resistance interests. The Enclave scouting thread emerges through contradictory information and road infrastructure; not a confirmed canon organization in 2281.
4. Decisions: choices change regional stability, trade, lives and wartime logistics. Original conditional ending slides and an optional, carefully validated bridge to vanilla endgame flags. The player can return to the Mojave before Hoover Dam.

Initial planning range: 6–8 distinct regions, 80–120 discoverable locations, 30–40 hours of content including optional exploration, 150–250 named NPCs, 20–30 original voice profiles and thousands of voiced lines. Tune after a vertical slice, performance tests, and author review.
## Visual identity
Primary title ROUTE 66 / FLAGSTAFF; Courier's Road is a working subtitle. Retro pre-War green highway exit sign, distressed enamel, reflective white lettering, highway shield and exit arrow. No invented historical exit number; use fictional sign details when appropriate. Architecture: mid-century motor courts, roadside atomic-age attractions, fortified civic ruins, ranching estates, reclaimed trade halls and Legion symbols. Avoid modern brands and present-day references.
